    Thesis · Draft, October 2026

    Personal AI can only own outcomes if someone modernizes the other side. So we build both sides, and the businesses we modernize bring us their clients.

    Every outcome has two sides.
    Today's AI covers one.

    Follow a single case from question to result, and see where each approach stops.

    The long tail won't
    modernize on its own.

    The biggest insurers, health systems and banks are already building for AI. The thousands of independent offices where most problems actually get stuck are not.

    Large institutions, modernizing themselvesMid-market and commercial operators: where we start

    Schematic, not to scale.

    Three bets.

    Two sides, one problem.

    Client cases reveal an operator's real workflow. Operator integration makes client outcomes reliable. Doing both is cheaper than doing either alone.

    Personal is business.

    Practice owners, landlords, trustees and shop owners run their business by text, email and phone. They won't buy enterprise software. A personal agent fits them.

    Operators distribute.

    Partners offer Onus as a member benefit, and members invite friends. The exclusivity is real because it works best where we're partnered.

    Members bring the next partner.

    When a case hits an office that isn't on Onus, that office becomes a lead, with data on how many of its clients got stuck and where.
