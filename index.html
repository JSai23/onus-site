Thesis · Draft, October 2026

Personal AI can only own outcomes if someone modernizes the other side. So we build both sides, and the businesses we modernize bring us their clients.

1 · The problem

Personal AI looks magical online.
Most real cases still fail.

Offices are built for people. Their portals, forms and phone menus need steps AI is unreliable at, and teaching AI to brute-force every office's system costs more per case than the case is worth. Even AI talking to an office's AI phone agent stalls, because the two sides were never built to work together.

And nobody is fixing both halves.

Consumer AI stops at the office. Office software stops at the front door.

2 · The insight

Modernization is easy
when you control both ends.

When one party runs the consumer's agent and the office's intake, there's nothing to translate. The request arrives in exactly the form the office needs, and every case teaches us how that office and that kind of problem work.

It doesn't depend on owning the app.

iMessage is the front door, not the product. The connection to the offices is the product, so it works through any interface.

3 · The market

The mid-market can't buy
what the big players build.

Large enterprises build AI in-house because their scale pays for it. The small law firm and the family dental practice can't. Building costs too much, enterprise software is built for someone else, and they watch the big dental group and the hospital system pull ahead.

Illustrative, not measured.

Who we serve first

Independent and group practices, insurance agencies, CPA and wealth offices, HOA and property managers, small law firms. Mid-market and commercial, in health, money and home.

Personal is business here

Owner-operators run their business by text, email and phone, mixed in with their personal lives. They won't buy enterprise software. A personal agent fits them.

4 · The product

One product, two sides.

For members

  • Text a problem, Onus opens a case
  • Works with trusted partners, follows up with everyone else
  • Live pages, bookings, reminders and approvals right in the chat
  • Closes only when the result is real

For partners

  • Routine requests resolve on their own
  • Everything else arrives complete and ready to act on
  • The partner decides where that line sits
  • Under their brand, or through hosted Onus
